Uptest offers a comprehensive range of non-destructive testing (NDT) services performed by qualified and experienced inspectors. We utilize internationally recognized inspection methods to detect flaws, assess material integrity, and ensure compliance with applicable standards without damaging the inspected component.

Inspection Methods

VT – Visual Testing

Visual testing (VT) is used to identify surface flaws and irregularities using the naked eye or optical aids such as magnifying glasses, mirrors, or endoscopes.

MT – Magnetic Particle Testing

Magnetic particle testing (MT) is used to detect surface and near-surface flaws in ferromagnetic materials, primarily carbon steel and ferritic steel.

PT – Penetrant Testing

Penetrant testing (PT) is used to detect open surface flaws in both ferrous and non-ferrous materials. The method is particularly effective for identifying cracks, porosity, and other surface defects.

ET – Eddy Current Testing

Eddy current testing (ET) is used to detect surface and near-surface flaws, including defects under paint or protective coatings. The method can also be used to measure electrical conductivity, material thickness, and coating thickness.

UT – Ultrasonic Testing

Ultrasonic testing (UT) utilizes high-frequency sound waves to detect internal and surface-breaking flaws, measure material thickness, and evaluate the integrity of weld joints and components.

Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT) & TOFD

Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT) and Time of Flight Diffraction (TOFD) are advanced ultrasonic methods that provide highly accurate detection, sizing, and characterization of internal and surface-breaking flaws. These methods can often serve as an alternative to radiographic testing (RT), delivering detailed inspection results without the use of ionizing radiation.
