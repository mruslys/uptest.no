About Us

Uptest is a company of highly qualified professionals with many years of experience in rope access and non-destructive testing (NDT). Most of our experience has been gained in the Scandinavian offshore oil and gas industry, where safety and quality standards are among the highest in the world.

Our expertise, commitment to safety, and focus on quality enable us to deliver reliable inspection and access solutions across a wide range of industries.

Quality Policy

UAB Uptest specialises in non-destructive testing (visual inspection, eddy current testing, magnetic particle testing, ultrasonic testing, and liquid penetrant testing) as well as work at height and industrial rope access.

To ensure continuous improvement, we have implemented a Quality Management System that complies with the ISO 9001 standard.

Our highest priorities are:

  • Meeting and exceeding customer expectations.
  • Ensuring the highest standards of safety.
  • Delivering reliable and high-quality services.

We place particular emphasis on:

  • Employee competence and continuous training.
  • Careful evaluation of suppliers and subcontractors.
  • Effective quality control.
  • Efficient and timely project delivery.

Each year, we establish quality objectives and work systematically to achieve them.

We are committed to:

  • Complying with the requirements of our Quality Management System and all applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Continually improving the effectiveness of our Quality Management System.
  • Providing the resources necessary to ensure the effective operation and continuous improvement of the Quality Management System.
