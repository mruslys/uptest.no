At Uptest, we are continuously expanding our operations and always welcome driven, safety-focused professionals to join our team. We specialize in industrial rope access and high-precision non-destructive testing (NDT), maintaining the rigorous standards expected in demanding environments.

Who We Are Looking For

Experienced Specialists

We regularly welcome certified industrial climbers (IRATA/SPRAT) and qualified NDT inspectors looking for challenging projects and professional growth.

Beginners & Enthusiasts

We also provide unique opportunities for enthusiastic newcomers to start their career, receive proper guidance, and test their capabilities in our field of operations.
